## Placeholders

- Ships, ocean, and sails are **procedural Three.js geometry** (no third-party meshes yet).
- Ambient audio uses **generated WAV data-URIs** as stand-ins until credited SFX packs are added.
- No commercial textures or paid audio are bundled in this scaffold.
